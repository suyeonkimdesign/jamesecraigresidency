The James Craig Residency is open to gifted designers, typographers, and writers—whether emerging or established—who are seeking a quiet, intentional space to work and reflect.

Applications will open in late 2026. Prospective applicants will be asked to submit a portfolio or writing sample, a statement of intent, and a brief bio. The Trust will select award recipients based on clarity of vision, dedication to craft, and alignment with the spirit of the residency.

Thank you for taking the time to complete this questionnaire. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us shape a meaningful and well-structured residency program for artists and writers. The residency is currently in development, and your insights will guide us as we refine the program’s format, amenities, and areas in which flexibility is still possible before launch.

This form is designed to be respectful of your time while still allowing for thoughtful, detailed responses. Most questions include multiple-choice options, with space for additional comments whenever you wish to elaborate.

Certain aspects of the residency, such as its location, are already established, but many other components remain open to development. Your input will help us determine what modifications or enhancements would best support future residents. A final, optional section invites respondents with prior residency experience to share reflections that may help us build upon successful models and avoid common pitfalls.

We anticipate offering our first trial residencies in Spring 2026 and deeply appreciate your contribution to this planning process. Your perspective will help us build a program that is both supportive and inspiring for its participants.
