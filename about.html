This annual residency welcomes up to two award recipients each year for a 10-day summer retreat, grounded in the principles of rest, reflection, and focused creative practice. With nature as its backdrop, the residency invites guests to engage deeply with their craft in an atmosphere of peace and solitude.

Explore The Estate Program Schedule

Award recipients are hosted at a private residence immersed in flora and fauna. The estate includes the facilities below:

Shared Studio

A sun-filled studio space on the ground floor with a home library filled with books on design, typography, and literature, along with archival ephemera

Garden & Terrace

A mix of private and shared terraces that look out into an open view of the surrounding nature

Hidden Oasis

Winding trails, bridges, and ponds that are all privately owned filled with flora and fauna

Private Bedroom

A spacious, private room with large windows and nature views
